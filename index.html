Abstract

RECAST recasts what a VLM knows into an Actionable Cost map (AC map). From the frame, the goal, and any constraint the user states, the VLM makes four judgments: traversability, caution, goal direction, and passable gaps. Expert vision models ground them into geometry on one cost map. The decoder is conditioned on that map and every candidate trajectory is scored on it, so reasoning and motion cannot misalign.

On a real quadruped, RECAST beats the strongest baseline by 31.4 success points with the lowest collision rate of any method, without retraining.

RECAST Pipeline

The VLM recasts the pivot frame (red) every 1.5 s. The current AC map, the candidates, and their scores are rebuilt on the live frame (green) every 0.36 s. Rejected candidates are red, the executed one green.
